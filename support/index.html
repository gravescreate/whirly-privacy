What's free, and what's Whirly Plus?

Every day you get one free 1-minute whirly or one game round, today's heart readings, and your last 7 days of History. Whirly Plus adds 3 and 5 minute whirlies, as many sessions as you like, every stress and heart rate trend, and your full History.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Apple manages Whirly Plus. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and Whirly. You can also open it from Whirly's Settings under Whirly Plus. Cancel at least 24 hours before your plan renews to avoid the next charge.

I bought Whirly Plus, but it's locked

Open Whirly's Settings (the gear at the top right of Today) and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My heart data isn't showing

Whirly reads heart rate and heart rate variability from Apple Health, so a watch or other wearable needs to be saving them there. Check access in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Whirly. New readings can take a while to sync from your watch. No wearable? That's okay: Whirly works the same without one.

My reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are on for Whirly in your phone's Settings, and that a Focus mode isn't silencing them. You can change your whirly times in Whirly's Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Everything Whirly records stays on your phone, so deleting the app removes it. Mindful sessions saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

Need more support?

Whirly is for everyday calm, not a substitute for professional care. If you're in crisis in the US, call or text 988, or contact local emergency services.
