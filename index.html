Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Whirly keeps what it knows about you on your phone. We don't run servers that collect your sessions or heart data, and we don't use ads or analytics.

Whirly is made by Whirly LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Whirly app on iPhone and Android: what it uses, where that information goes, and the choices you have.

The short version:

  • Your whirlies, settings and heart data stay on your phone.
  • Heart data is never sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • If you buy Whirly Plus, Apple or Google handles the payment, and RevenueCat confirms your subscription.
  • There are no accounts, ads or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Everything Whirly records is stored in the app on your phone. It isn't sent to us.

  • Your whirlies and games: which pattern or game, when, how long, and whether you finished. This is what your streak, Today list and History are built from.
  • Your settings: your goal, reminder times and app options.
  • Heart insights: the heart readings Whirly reads from Apple Health or Health Connect (see Health data below), and the personal averages it works out from them.
  • Reminders: these are scheduled on your phone. No server sends them.
  • Microphone: the Dandelion game uses the microphone only while you play, to measure how strongly you breathe out. The sound isn't saved, sent or used for anything else, and the game also works without it.

If you back up your phone to iCloud or Google, the backup may include Whirly's data. Those backups are controlled by your Apple or Google account, not by us.

Health data

Whirly uses health data only to show you your own stress and heart insights, and only if you allow it.

WhatRead or writeWhy
Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variabilityReadTo learn your normal levels, spot stressful moments and show how breathing helps
Steps and workoutsReadTo leave out exercise, so a run isn't mistaken for stress
Mindful sessionsWriteTo save each finished whirly to Apple Health or Health Connect

Health data is read from Apple Health on iPhone and Health Connect on Android. It is processed on your phone and never leaves it. We never sell it, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising or marketing.

You can change what Whirly reads or writes at any time: on iPhone in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Whirly, and on Android in the Health Connect app. Whirly keeps working without health data; only the heart insights stop.

Whirly Plus purchases

If you subscribe to Whirly Plus, the only information that leaves your phone is what's needed to process and confirm the purchase.

  • Apple or Google handles payment through your App Store or Google Play account. We never see your card or payment details. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • RevenueCat is the service we use to check whether you have Whirly Plus and to restore it on a new phone. It receives:
    • a random ID the app creates, which isn't linked to your name or email
    • your purchase and subscription details: plan, price, currency, country, dates and status
    • basic technical details: app version, device type and operating system, and the IP address that comes with any internet request

RevenueCat never receives your whirlies, settings or health data. It handles this information under its own privacy policy. We use it only to show the right Whirly Plus status in the app and to help with subscription questions.

You can cancel or change your subscription at any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

What we don't do

  • No accounts: you never sign up or give us your name or email to use Whirly.
  • No analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • No ads.
  • No selling or renting personal information to anyone.

Your choices

  • Permissions: you can turn off health access, the microphone or notifications at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Deleting your data: deleting Whirly removes everything it stored on your phone. Mindful sessions already saved to Apple Health or Health Connect stay there until you delete them in that app.
  • Your purchase records: to ask us to delete the subscription records RevenueCat holds for your random ID, email us. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records.

Children

Whirly isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your information is used, we'll also mention it in the app or its App Store notes.
